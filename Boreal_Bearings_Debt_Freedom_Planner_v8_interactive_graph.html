How this version handles payments

  • Each debt follows its own recurring minimum-payment schedule.
  • Monthly payments recur on the same day of the month (or the month's last day when needed).
  • Twice-monthly extra payments occur on the 1st and 15th.
  • When a debt is cleared, its scheduled minimum payment is redirected to the current priority debt on subsequent scheduled dates.
  • Extra payments are separate from minimum payments and follow the extra-payment schedule you choose.
